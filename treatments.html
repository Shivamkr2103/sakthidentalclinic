Teeth Cleaning & Scaling

Professional cleaning removes plaque and tartar buildup to support gum health. Depending on the condition, your dentist may recommend scaling, deeper cleaning or other periodontal care.

Ask about this treatment ↗
＋

Tooth Filling

Fillings can restore teeth affected by cavities, minor damage or wear. Your dentist will assess the tooth and recommend suitable materials and treatment to help preserve natural structure.

Ask about this treatment ↗
◈

Tooth Extraction

When a tooth cannot be restored, extraction may be recommended after assessment. The team explains the procedure and aftercare to help make the process as comfortable as possible.

Ask about this treatment ↗
▱

Artificial Complete Denture

Custom complete dentures can replace a full arch of missing teeth, helping restore appearance and daily function. Fit and design are planned around individual needs.

Ask about this treatment ↗
⌁

Dental Implants

Dental implants can replace missing teeth using an implant-supported restoration. Suitability depends on oral health, bone support and an individualized clinical assessment.

Ask about this treatment ↗
☼

Laser Dentistry

Laser technology may be used for selected soft-tissue and gum procedures. Your dentist can explain the potential benefits, limitations and whether a laser approach is appropriate.

Ask about this treatment ↗
⌘

Root Canal Therapy

Root canal treatment addresses infection or inflammation inside a tooth. The dentist cleans and seals the internal space to help relieve symptoms and preserve the tooth when possible.

Ask about this treatment ↗
◒

Wisdom Tooth Extraction

Wisdom teeth may need removal when impacted, infected or causing other problems. Assessment and aftercare are tailored to the patient's condition.

Ask about this treatment ↗
▤

Fixed Partial Denture (Bridge)

A dental bridge replaces one or more missing teeth by attaching a replacement tooth to supporting teeth or implants, where clinically suitable.

Ask about this treatment ↗
✦

Teeth Whitening (Bleaching)

Professional whitening may help lighten certain types of tooth discoloration. A dental assessment helps identify the cause of staining and suitable options.

Ask about this treatment ↗
◇

Veneers

Veneers are custom-made coverings used to improve the appearance of selected teeth. Your dentist can discuss suitability, preparation and maintenance.

Ask about this treatment ↗
♡

Pediatric Dentistry

Gentle dental care for children focuses on check-ups, prevention, early assessment and helping young patients build positive oral-health habits.

Ask about this treatment ↗
⌖

Flap Surgery

For selected advanced gum conditions, periodontal flap surgery may help access areas that require deeper cleaning. A dentist will explain the risks and alternatives.

Ask about this treatment ↗
⌁

Orthodontic Braces

Braces can gradually improve tooth alignment and bite. Options may include metal, ceramic or other appliances, depending on clinical needs.

Ask about this treatment ↗
▱

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are removable transparent trays used to move teeth gradually in suitable cases. Results depend on the treatment plan and consistent use.

Ask about this treatment ↗
✧

Bleaching

A cosmetic dental whitening option for certain stains and discoloration. A consultation helps determine whether whitening is suitable and what results may be realistic.
