FOUNDER & DENTIST

Get to know Dr. Anupriya.

Dr. Anupriya began her professional journey after graduating in 2000. During six years at Mathura Clinic, she refined her clinical skills and deepened her commitment to patient wellbeing.

In 2004, she established Sakthi Dental Clinic in Hosur with a vision to make quality dental care accessible. Her work has also included service as a dental consultant with the Primary Health Center at Chandara Hospital.

At Sakthi Dental Clinic, the team aims to combine contemporary dental technology with a welcoming environment. Treatment is tailored to each patient's needs.
