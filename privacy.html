Our commitment

At Sakthi Dental Clinic, we are committed to respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and disclosed when you visit this website or contact the clinic through it.

Information we collect

We may receive information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, phone number and message when you submit an enquiry or request an appointment. Basic technical information may also be processed by the website host, such as browser type, device information and server logs.

How we use information

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, coordinate appointment requests, provide information you ask for, maintain website security and improve the website. Please do not submit detailed medical records or sensitive health information through a general website form.

Cookies and third-party services

This website may use third-party services such as website hosting, embedded maps or web fonts. Those providers may process technical data under their own policies. Any analytics or advertising cookies should only be added after the clinic has reviewed the relevant consent requirements.

Sharing and retention

We do not intend to sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the website or respond to your request, or when disclosure is required by law. Information should be retained only as long as necessary for the stated purpose and applicable obligations.

Security

Reasonable safeguards should be used to protect personal information. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. The contact form in this demo is front-end only and does not send or store submissions.

Your choices and rights

You may contact the clinic to ask about personal information you have provided or request correction or deletion where applicable. Requests may be subject to legal and operational requirements.

Children’s privacy

This website is intended to provide information about family dental services. A parent or guardian should submit contact or appointment requests on behalf of a child.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact info@sakthidentalclinic.in or write to B2/8, SBM Layout, Anthivadi, Hosur, Tamil Nadu 635109, India.
