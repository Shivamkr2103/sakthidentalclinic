FREQUENTLY ASKED

Good to know
before you visit.

These answers are for general information and cannot replace advice from your dentist. Every patient's needs are different.

Ask us a question ↗
Can medication completely relieve tooth pain?+

Medication may provide temporary relief, but lasting relief depends on identifying and treating the underlying cause. Seek dental advice for ongoing or severe pain.

Does teeth scaling damage enamel or create gaps?+

Professional scaling is intended to remove plaque and tartar and does not create gaps. Temporary sensitivity can occur. Your dentist can explain what to expect for your gums and teeth.

Can fluorosis stains be removed through scaling?+

Scaling can remove surface deposits and stains, but fluorosis is an internal change in tooth enamel. A dentist can discuss cosmetic options that may be suitable.

Can painful teeth be treated with fillings?+

Not always. If decay or damage affects the pulp inside the tooth, root canal treatment or another option may be needed. An examination determines the right treatment.

Is a crown necessary after root canal treatment?+

A crown is often recommended for teeth that need extra protection after root canal treatment, but the decision depends on the tooth and the amount of remaining structure.

Can milk teeth need root canal treatment?+

Yes. Some baby teeth with deep decay may benefit from pulp treatment to manage infection and preserve the tooth until it is naturally ready to fall out.

How can I maintain good oral hygiene?+

Brush twice a day with fluoride toothpaste, clean between teeth daily, limit frequent sugary snacks and attend routine dental check-ups.

What is the best age to get braces?+

The right time depends on growth, tooth position and bite. An orthodontic assessment can identify whether early evaluation or treatment is appropriate.

Do all wisdom teeth need to be removed?+

No. Removal is usually considered when wisdom teeth cause or are likely to cause problems, such as infection, impaction or damage to nearby teeth.

Is it important to replace missing teeth after extraction?+

Replacing missing teeth may help restore chewing and prevent unwanted movement in some cases. Your dentist can discuss the benefits and alternatives for your situation.

What are the benefits of dental implants?+

Implants can provide support for replacement teeth and restore aspects of chewing and appearance. Suitability and outcomes vary, so a clinical assessment is essential.

Which toothbrush and toothpaste should I use?+

A soft-bristled brush and fluoride toothpaste are suitable for many people. Ask your dentist for advice tailored to your age, gums and dental needs.

What causes tooth sensitivity?+

Sensitivity can be related to enamel wear, decay, exposed roots, cracks or other issues. A dentist can identify the cause and recommend appropriate care.

How often should I see a dentist?+

Many people benefit from check-ups about every six months, but the appropriate interval depends on individual oral-health risks and your dentist's advice.
